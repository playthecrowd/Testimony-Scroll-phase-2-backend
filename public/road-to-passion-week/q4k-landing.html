What Every Host Gets

Your Q4K Host Plot Brings Every Lesson to Life

Four connected zones — lobby, game tower, drop-off, and reward tower — give every church or group a ready-made Bible gaming world.

1

Lobby Starting Point

A branded entry area where players begin, view screens, check leaderboards, and prepare for the quest.

2

Livestream Lesson Screen

Stream speakers, pastors, teachers, or youth leaders directly into the gameplay experience.

3

Game Portals & Question Objects

Players enter Bible missions, trivia challenges, and scavenger hunts, then search, collect, and match objects tied to the lesson.

4

Drop-Off Zone & Reward Tower

Players submit collected items to complete challenges, then race up the reward stair tower to claim badges and prizes before time runs out.

5

Sponsor Reward Areas

Sponsors support prizes, game credits, badges, featured assets, and youth access throughout the plot.

The Road to Easter

Seven monthly themes carry players from discipleship in the fall to the full Passion of Christ experience at Easter.

September

Follow Me: Discipleship Begins

Church outreach, pop-up demos, and speaker nights focused on calling, identity, and following Jesus.

October

Stand Firm: Faith Under Pressure

Missions about courage, spiritual choices, peer pressure, and standing firm in faith.

November

Kingdom Builders: Gratitude & Service

Gameplay centered on gratitude, generosity, teamwork, and using your gifts for kingdom purpose.

December

The Promise Arrives

A Christmas-centered experience brings the birth of Jesus to life through live storytelling and game previews.

January

Early Access Opens

Hosts unlock customizable environments, church-connected plots, badges, and multiplayer missions.

February

Connected Kingdom Communities

Hosts connect environments side by side so multiple churches can play and build shared experiences.

March / Easter

The Passion Quest

The full Easter experience: live gameplay, VR, mixed reality, music, speakers, and powerful storytelling.
